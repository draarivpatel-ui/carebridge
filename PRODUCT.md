# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and browser ES modules. No runtime packages or application server.

## Users

Family members and neighbors practicing how to split a week of oncology or recovery logistics. The hackathon build uses fictional scenarios only and does not collect patient records.

## Product Purpose

Make the gaps and the uneven workload visible, let the group rehearse a dropout, then give each person a limited handoff that can leave the browser as a calendar export, a printed sheet or a shareable link.

## Constraints

Clinical exercises teach the limits of individual rules. They never diagnose, change medications, or replace the care team's plan. The print handoff is available in English and Spanish. Keyboard shortcuts start off.
