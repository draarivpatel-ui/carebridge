    What the numbers mean

    Call on the first reading
    During chemotherapy, a single reading at or above 100.4 °F or 38.0 °C means call the care team now; do not wait an hour to see whether it holds.
    The formal definitions, for comparison
    Oral readings only: one reading at or above 101.0 °F or 38.3 °C, or a reading at or above 100.4 °F or 38.0 °C held for one full hour. The published cutoffs are stated per unit and are not exact conversions of each other.

    Nothing matching a rule is not an all-clear and no result here is a diagnosis; keep watching and call the team if anything changes. This demo never suggests doses.

    References for this exercise include the CDC's Watch Out for Fever guidance and the IDSA clinical guideline on fever and neutropenia.
